Privacy

This is Pearla. Public privacy policy: pearla.net/privacy.

Recovery phrases, seeds, private keys, and passwords stay on this device, encrypted locally. Sessions only store unlock expiry.

Address queries, fee rates, and signed transactions you approve are sent only to the indexer you chose under Network (default blockbook.pearlresearch.ai). Passwords and recovery phrases are not included.

Home USD totals try Coinbase PEARL-USD first, then CoinGecko on failure; those requests do not include addresses, balances, or keys.

We do not read browsing history or inject APIs into web pages.

Support Pearla
